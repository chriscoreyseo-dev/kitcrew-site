Questions

The ones everyone asks.

Honest answers, no hedging. If your question isn't here, email ceo@kitfire.ai.

Will KitCrew make income claims for me?

Never — and that's a feature, not a limitation. Income claims are blocked at the system level in everything a KitCrew agent produces, not just prompted around. Your business stays clean and your company's compliance team stays happy.

Will KitCrew work with my company?

KitCrew is built for network marketers and affiliates in any company. Onboarding reads your replicated site and your company's own published product line, and your agents are trained on it — your products, your language, your links.

Do the agents post or message without asking me first?

Not until you say so. Every agent starts approve-first: it drafts, you approve with a tap. As an agent earns a track record with you, you can graduate individual actions to run automatically — and pull that back any time.

Will my content sound like AI wrote it?

Your Content Agent studies how you actually write and talk, and drafts in that style. You approve everything, so nothing goes out that doesn't sound like something you decided to say.

Do I need to be a MarketHive member to use KitCrew?

No. KitCrew works for any network marketer or affiliate. If you are on MarketHive and hold Entrepreneur One, KitCrew builds out your MarketHive presence free, one time, on top of your subscription — see the MarketHive page for what's included.

How does the referral dashboard work?

Every member gets a personal referral link. Refer people and your back office shows your organization several levels deep, with figures based only on payments actually collected — real numbers on your own dashboard, never a projection. You see your own numbers only, never anyone else's.

I'm an affiliate marketer, not in MLM — is KitCrew for me?

Yes. Set team-building aside and affiliate marketing is capture, follow-up, content, and engagement — exactly what four of the six agents do all day. The Duplication Agent simply waits until you have a team to duplicate.

What does KitCrew cost?

One plan: Duplicator Pro, $299/mo or $2,990/yr (two months free), 1 seat, all six agents included. No tiers to decode and no extra-seat pricing. See pricing on the home page.

Who actually builds and runs KitCrew?

KitCrew is a product of KitFire AI Inc, based in Flushing, Michigan. One company builds it, hosts it, and runs it — KitCrew is not a co-branded product with any network marketing company or platform.
