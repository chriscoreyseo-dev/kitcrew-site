Common questions

What does this cost?
Nothing. The review is free and there is no obligation. If a product like an annuity ever fits your goals and you choose to move forward, the licensed professional is compensated by the insurance company — never out of your pocket for the review.
Is this connected to my employer or 401(k) provider?
No. This is an independent review by a licensed insurance professional. It is not affiliated with, or endorsed by, your employer, your plan administrator, or any government agency.
Will I be pressured to move my money?
No. The review is educational — you'll get a clear picture of fees, risk, and options, and every decision stays yours. Many people simply keep the findings and change nothing.
What should I have ready for the call?
Your most recent 401(k) statement is helpful but not required. Even just knowing roughly what you have and where it's held is enough to start.
What is "guaranteed lifetime income"?
Certain insurance products, such as annuities, can convert part of your savings into a payment stream guaranteed for life. Guarantees are backed by the financial strength and claims-paying ability of the issuing insurance company. Whether one fits depends on your situation — that's part of what the review covers.
