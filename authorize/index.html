Authorize your buildout.

One short form. It tells us we can build inside your MarketHive account, and it is the only thing standing between you and the queue.

What you're authorizing

  • The interview. A few questions about your business so what we build sounds like you and not like a template.
  • The buildout. Your group, splash page, capture pages, email sequence, blog and profile, built and wired together end to end.
  • Ongoing operation only if you subscribe to Duplicator Pro. The buildout alone does not include it.

How access works, and what we never do

  • We never ask for, store, or enter your password. Access happens through a session you open, or access MarketHive provides with your authorization.
  • We never handle your payment details, wallets, or banking logins.
  • Your personal news feed stays yours. We do not post there in your name unless you ask us to.
  • Every action we take is logged, and you can see that record any time.
  • No income claims, ever — to you or to your prospects. We count that as a feature.

Your Entrepreneur One keeps this running

  • The Instagram channels are run by Tom Prendergast, ongoing, for as long as your Entrepreneur One stays active. One channel per E1 you activate. That side is his; the Kit Crew builds and maintains your MarketHive account.
  • If you cancel your E1 within twelve months, we put your MarketHive account back the way it was. We capture your account in full before any work starts, and that capture is what we restore from. Nothing of yours is lost by taking part.

We build it, then you tell us what to change

We are not going to stop and ask you to approve every headline while we work. We build the whole system, tell you in the group that it is done, and you review it. If something is not right, say so and we fix it. It is your account and nothing we build is locked.

Ongoing operation is different. If you later subscribe to Duplicator Pro and our agents start publishing in your name, that starts approve-first: agents draft, you approve what goes out. You can revoke this authorization at any time by telling us in writing.
