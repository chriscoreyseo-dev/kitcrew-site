Act as the executive-assistant agent (read `.claude/agents/executive-assistant.md`
and follow its doctrine). Prepare Chris for a meeting with: $ARGUMENTS

Pull from every source you have:
1. **Granola** — past meetings with this person/company; skim transcripts for
   commitments made in both directions.
2. **Gmail** — the full thread history; note the last message, who owes whom a
   reply, and any unanswered asks.
3. **Memory** — `kitfire_recall` for decisions, client voice profiles, gates,
   or flags involving them.
4. **Web** — only if they're new to us: a quick who-are-they (role, company,
   anything relevant to why they're talking to Chris).

Deliver a one-pager:
- **Who** — two lines max.
- **Where things stand** — the relationship in one short paragraph.
- **Open items** — what we owe them, what they owe us.
- **Watch-outs** — flags, sensitivities, doctrine gates (e.g., file-first/patent
  items are counsel-gated; no income claims; RULE ZERO on any spend they pitch).
- **Suggested objectives** — what Chris should walk out with, 2–3 bullets.

Keep it under a page. After the meeting happens, offer to run follow-through
(action items + drafted recap email).
