# Zeely (zeely.ai) — 2026-08-22

**Verdict: STEAL.** Failed **G5 (redundancy)** — the creative half duplicates
the Higgsfield rail already connected to these sessions. G6 and G8 also flag.

Arrived as a Meta ad link (Instagram Feed, broad US, purchase-optimized)
while evaluating promotion for the $97 `/course/` offer.

## What it is
AI ad factory + Meta campaign launcher for SMBs with no creative team. Product
link in, static ads (~100 templates) and avatar video ads (~500 avatars) out,
then it launches and manages the Meta campaign without Ads Manager.

## True cost (G3)
- Starter $29.95 / Plus $49.95 / Growth $79.95 per month — **tiers buy credits
  only, features identical** (2,500 / 5,000 / 10,000)
- **+12% of Meta ad spend**, halved to 6% only if you buy the "Booster" add-on
- Entry is a paid trial ($19.95 / 2wk, $29.95 / 4wk, $49.95 / 8wk) that
  auto-renews into the monthly
- Credits-per-video **not published** — cost per asset is unmodelable before
  signing

## Exit cost (G4)
~4.2/5 on ~2,000+ Trustpilot reviews, but a consistent independent complaint
pattern across BBB / App Store / forums: trial-to-subscription billing, refund
friction, and users who deleted the app assuming that cancelled and were
charged for weeks. BBB shows repeat $49.95 charges and one case of eleven $119
charges refunded only after a complaint.

## Why it failed
- **G5** — creative atoms (avatar video, UGC, script, captions, 9x16, voice)
  are already owned via Higgsfield; Bram's 56s AI pitch audio already ships in
  the `/course/` hero. Paying sub + 12% for a thinner version of a capability
  on the bench is the wrong trade.
- **G6** — `/course/` is $97 with a $47 order bump. $144 AOV, one-time, no
  continuity. Cold Meta CPA for low-ticket info products routinely exceeds
  that. The constraint is the backend, not the ad tool.
- **G7** — network-marketing audience is Meta's hardest-enforced category
  (income claims, MLM recruitment angles), and the claims policy reads the
  **landing page**, not just ad copy.
- **G8** — campaign launching needs partner/asset access into a Meta Business
  portfolio. The InstaForge Meta app rides **Tom's** developer account
  (`S342-DRL-02`), so portfolio overlap must be established before any grant.
  Unresolved at time of ruling.

## The steal (G9)
Their own winning creative, readable straight off the ad URL: **faceless 9:16
Seedance video**, broad US, purchase-optimized, Instagram Feed. Reproducible
on Higgsfield for free. Recommended instead: 3–5 faceless 9:16 videos for
`/chapter-one/`, run through the virality predictor, posted organically.

## What would change the verdict
A backend/continuity offer behind the $97 that lifts LTV well above cold-Meta
CPA, **and** a resolved answer on Business-portfolio separation from Tom's
developer account. Absent both, redundancy alone still holds it at SKIP.

`zeely.ai`, Trustpilot and BBB were all blocked by the session egress proxy —
every vendor-side figure above is secondhand via search. Most "Zeely review"
pages in results (gethookd, tagshop, adsgen, ugcgen, vidmuse, ezugc) are
competing UGC tools; their negatives are discounted accordingly.
