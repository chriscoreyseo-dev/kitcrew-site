## KitCrew

Six named agents — Follow-Up, Content, Lead, Engagement, Duplication, Triage —
trained on what the member actually sells, drafting for approval.

- **Plan:** Duplicator Pro, $299/mo or $2,990/yr, 1 seat, all six agents.
- **Positioning:** the tool pile is what kills duplication, not the product.
- **Not affiliated** with, endorsed by, or a distributor of any network
  marketing company.
- Onboarding reads the member's replicated site and their company's published
  product line. Nothing invented.

## The course

$97 one payment, optional $47 Complete upgrade at checkout, 30-day refund.
Fifteen chapters in three parts plus the Prompt Vault (52 prompts in the house
pattern: a job, a No list, an answer format, a prove-it line).

Teaches the AI OS discipline — sessions and state, a decision log, verification
culture, and **chartering an AI seat: one property, hard boundaries, a written
escalation rule.**

Never cite a specific session count — "hundreds of logged sessions."

## Never Drop The Ball

$199 one-time, one account. Sets up the customer's own Instagram so people who
ask in comments get a fresh reply and the promised thing in DMs, with a list of
everyone who asked and who opened.

- Runs on **Instagram's own approved messaging tools, on the customer's
  account, with their permission.** Nothing logs in as them.
- The underlying messaging service is ~$13/mo, billed to the customer directly
  by that provider — not by KitFire.
- 30-day refund.

**This page is the house benchmark for offer construction** — it's the one
surface that carries a complete offer (promise, guarantee, objection-plug,
internal scarcity). Use it as the voice template.

## MarketHive build-out

Free, one time, with the member's own Entrepreneur One package ($100/mo paid by
them directly to MarketHive — MarketHive's tier, not a KitFire product). No
KitCrew subscription required. **Capped at the first 100 new or reactivated E1
packages.**

Delivers: profile header, blog group, group capture page, group splash page,
email autoresponders, comment monitoring wired to the Engagement Agent.

Checklist-scoped — when the deliverables are live it is complete. Ongoing
running is a separate Duplicator Pro subscription and is never required.

## Video prompt playbook

Free at `/prompts/`. The eleven-block structure for cinematic AI video and the
rules that stop generated footage looking fake. Currently funnels to a nav
anchor rather than any offer.

## Social Brain

At `/socialbrain/`. Enter platform, category, posting time and a draft caption;
get back what comparable posts actually got — engagement, views, likes,
comments, shares, across platforms.

**Currently linked from nowhere** — not in the nav, not in `sitemap.xml`, not
referenced by any page. It works and no customer can find it.
